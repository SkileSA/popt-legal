## Your recovery code

Popt encrypts your content with keys that only your devices hold. The one way to set up a new device, or to get back in after losing every signed-in device, is your **recovery code**, generated on your device and never seen by us.

**If you lose your recovery code and have no signed-in device left, your content is gone, and we cannot bring it back.** Not with a password reset, not with a support request, not with a court order. We have no copy of your keys, by design, and that is what makes the encryption real. Keep the code somewhere safe, outside Popt. The app also keeps a copy in your iCloud Keychain when iCloud Keychain is on, which is why signing back in on one of your Apple devices does not always cause Popt to request the recovery code.

We are not liable for content that becomes unreadable because a recovery code was lost.
